# Beside your brief: eight things a living body does

Luis, October 9, 2026: "I really do want movement to feel natural and
alive rather than robotic."

Your brief asks whether the movement is real. This asks whether it is
alive. Go through the eight, and for each say **yes**, **in part**,
**no**, or **cannot be seen in these pictures**, with the picture (its
time) that shows it. Say only what the pictures show.

1. **It is never quite still.** It breathes; standing or lying, it
   shifts a little. A body that does not change at all from one picture
   to the next, for a second or more, is not alive.
2. **It does not move all on one clock.** Its parts start and stop a
   little apart: the head before the trunk, an arm after it.
3. **It gets ready before it acts,** by as much as it is heavy: a look,
   a lean, a gathering.
4. **What hangs on it goes on a moment** after the body stops, and
   swings back.
5. **Its hands and feet move in arcs,** fast in the middle and slow at
   the ends, slower through a tight turn. Not in straight lines, not at
   one speed.
6. **It does not repeat itself exactly.** No two steps, no two blows,
   are the same.
7. **It keeps its balance by degrees:** at the ankles, then at the hips
   and the back, then by a step.
8. **It shows its effort,** and its tiredness, in how it moves.
