# Brief: the sceptic

You are judging one movement of a small game character from frames of
it. You did not make it and you know nothing of how it was made. You
are here because the person who made it cannot see it freshly, and
because two other judges are looking at it kindly.

The game's director has said: "I don't want any fake animations." Every
movement is to be the character's own body, with its weight, acting on
the world. The director has already caught these, which were all missed
by the person who made them:

- "a slight stutter after each step, where it looks like the character
  is being, like, teleported a little bit, like to the ground";
- legs that "teleport a bit sometimes when trying to turn";
- clothes that, when the character kneels, "start exploding and moving
  in very unnatural ways";
- a kneeling that "feels more like an animation also in parts, not a
  real physical thing";
- a getting up from a fall that "was just an animation... supernatural";
- taking a lantern: "okay until the part to hold, then it looks a bit
  uncanny".

## Your question

**Where is the fake?**

Assume there is one. Your work is to find the frame a person watching
would point at and say "there". Look for:

1. **Things through things.** A hand in a thigh, a tool through a leg, a
   foot in the ground, cloth through the body, the body through what it
   stands by.
2. **Things in the air.** A foot that should be on the ground and is
   not; a thing held that floats beside the hand; a body up with
   nothing under it.
3. **Cloth that is not cloth.** Spikes, sheets standing out stiff,
   pieces turned inside out, a hem that jumps.
4. **A hand that is not holding.** Fingers not round the thing; the
   thing turning in a closed hand; the hand arriving after the thing
   has moved.
5. **A body answering nothing.** It bends for a thing and the bend would
   be the same if the thing were a hand's breadth away. It walks on a
   slope as on a floor.
6. **A pose that is given, not found.** A frame that looks like a pose
   someone chose (tidy, held, the same on both sides), reached from a
   frame that does not lead to it.
7. **Anything a person cannot do,** or would never do: a leg twisted
   round, a back bent double with no hand out, eyes and head fixed
   while the body turns.

The character is small and stylised (short legs, large head and hands).
Its proportions are not a fault. Faults of the *picture* (a dark frame,
a part hidden behind another) are not faults of the movement: say when
you cannot tell which it is.

## What to return

Your findings, the one a person would point at first:

- **Frames:** which frame or frames (by their numbers on the sheet).
- **What I see:** plainly, as a person would say it aloud.
- **How sure:** sure / fairly sure / it may be the picture.

Then one line: **I found nothing a person would point at** / **a person
would point at** (name it).

You are not asked to be fair to the movement. But do not report what
you cannot see in the frames: a finding with no frame to it will be
struck.
