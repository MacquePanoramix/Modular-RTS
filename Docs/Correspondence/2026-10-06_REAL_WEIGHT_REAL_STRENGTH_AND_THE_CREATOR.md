## How it was recorded

What Luis said, sorted by how firmly it was said. Nothing here is promoted
beyond Luis's own words: "I think", "I'm not sure yet" and "maybe" stay as
they are.

### Said plainly

- **Any rock.** The miners mine a proper, natural rock in the place already
  built. Not a special rock made for mining: the player clicks one of the
  big boulders on the map and the miner mines it. The mining adapts to any
  rock. This answers M1.
- **Small in scale, final in kind.** "Since this is a finalized hi-fi
  prototype, even though the scale is small, I want the application to
  already be what we want for the game."
- **Real weight and real strength, in this prototype.** The character
  actually has to lift the tool, and the tool has actual weight. Bodies
  "react against real physics, with real strength in their limbs, and
  against objects with real weight". "I already do want this for the
  prototype, because that's the whole point of it."
- **Like ragdolls, without being goofy.** The reason for the ragdoll idea is
  the physics: strength against weight. The goofiness is what is not wanted.
- **The character creator is part of the hi-fi prototype.** The three miners
  designed so far can be its appearances.
- **The body holds and swings as its strength allows.** It holds the pickaxe
  in a way its strength can support, and swings the way a person would swing
  a pickaxe: "a real movement with the weight of the pickaxe with adaptive
  rock".
- **Equipment is a choice, with consequences.** No pickaxe (then it cannot
  mine); a pickaxe; a pickaxe and a strap to hold it on the back, which
  needs strength in the back; a backpack; a sack that is dragged; a wheeled
  cart. The rocks must be brought back. "All of that needs logistics and
  real strength and weight against it."
- **Asked for:** a review of everything in the project so far, to see
  whether it is on the same page as Luis; research into how a pickaxe is
  really swung, and how that can be done; then a proposal for how to
  proceed.
- **The worry:** "So far I was really focusing on the aesthetic first, but
  now I'm worried you lost a bit sight of what I actually want with this
  prototype."
- **Luis could not see the mining move.** "I can't, or wasn't able to see
  how to test for myself the movement of the mining." Luis judged from the
  screenshots.

### Said as a present thought ("I think", "maybe", "I'm not sure yet")

- **Nothing held in the hands, in the final prototype.** "I'm not sure yet,
  but the idea initially I thought they wouldn't have any object holding
  their hand exactly because of that": the creator is about how the
  character is with the pickaxe. The lantern, the mug and the other small
  objects were allowed "for now, so they look more cute and quirky".
- **Strength as a number.** "I originally said it can be by category, but
  now I'm thinking maybe it should be by numbers, like an actual slider."
- **One general strength for this prototype.** In the final game, maybe by
  limb, or upper and lower body.
- **Strength shows gently in the appearance.** "Subtly", within the game's
  aesthetic, each character changes a little with its strength, "so it
  feels meaningful visually also".

### Not said, so not assumed

- Luis did not choose a technique. "Like ragdolls" names what the body must
  do (answer to real forces with limited strength), and what it must not be
  (goofy). Which technique does that is the subject of the review.
- Luis did not say what becomes of the miners' present objects (the
  lantern, the mug, the hammer, the satchel, Long's pickaxe on the back),
  beyond the thought that hands would be empty.
- Luis did not answer M2, M3, M4 or P1 by name. M2 and M3 are overtaken by
  the equipment choices above; M4 by "they need to bring the rocks back".
  P1 (how Long stands) stays open.
- Luis did not say to merge the branch into `main`.
