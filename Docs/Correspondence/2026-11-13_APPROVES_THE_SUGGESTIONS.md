# Luis, November 13, 2026 (afternoon): the six suggestions approved

After the research on turning and settling (`Docs/Research/2026-11-13_TurningAndSettling/research.md`) was sent to
him with a recommendation on each of its six questions, verbatim:

> Okay I generally think I approve all your suggestions. Good job. Just make sure to always do research into the best
> ways to move forward and try to let everything keep following all our design philosophies of both mechanics and
> aesthetics for the game. Thanks a lot for your work. You are doing great!

The recommendations he approved, as they were sent:

1. Tie the body's turn to the steps (most of a sharp turn during one stance, prepared by the step before), instead
   of a steady rate up to 420 degrees a second: yes. It is the biggest difference from people.
2. A planted leg steps round sooner (about 35 degrees of hip twist instead of 62): yes, but first measured whether 62
   is what bends the legs.
3. The head slightly ahead of the hips: yes, kept small.
4. In sharp turns, the foot that is down decides between a wide side step and a pivot with the legs crossing, and the
   swinging foot goes round the standing leg instead of through it: yes.
5. After a caught shove, once the sway has died down, the miner steps back calmly into its own stance, taking more
   steps after a bigger push: yes, as an option next to what it does now (our own design: no study measures it).
6. Rarer standing shifts: no change to the idle he loves, unless he would like to see a rarer version next to it.

Also said with them: his answers decide the turning design, which is written up as a proposal for him to agree to
before anything is rebuilt.
