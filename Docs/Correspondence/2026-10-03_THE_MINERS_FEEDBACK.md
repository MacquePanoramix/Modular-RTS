# The miners — Luis's feedback — October 3, 2026

**From:** Luis (Game Director), after seeing the miners remade at higher
quality ([TheMiners.md](../ArtDirection/TheMiners.md)).

## Message (verbatim)

> I think they are almost perfect now! Honestly they are already adorable! I love their designs. The only things I still feel is, when more up close, it becomes a bit more uncanny their necks (like it becomes a bit too obvious that their heads looks glued into the body rather than being a normal neck coming from the clothes. Up close also I feel the separated feed designs feels a tiny bit strange. The hands of the round character also seem a tiny bit strange from the screenshot. Also take a deep look yourself at them from every distance to see if there's anything to further polish to make them nicer to look at. After that you can start the rigging process and making them light enough to run well on the rts. Also yes I was thinking of oferring all three as options for this prototype.

## How it was recorded

- **The miners' designs.** "Almost perfect", "already adorable". Luis loves
  their designs.
- **To polish:**
  - **Necks.** Up close the heads look glued onto the bodies; they should read
    as a normal neck coming out of the clothes.
  - **Feet.** Up close the separated feet look a little strange.
  - **Round's hands.** They look a little strange.
  - **A careful look at every distance** for anything else that would make
    them nicer to look at.
- **Then:**
  - rig them;
  - make them light enough to run well in the RTS.
- **All three in the prototype.** Luis decided to offer Small, Long and Round
  as choices in this prototype.
