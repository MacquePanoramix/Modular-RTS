## What it decides and asks

- **The flaps on their seams: on by default** (decided). "A lot still needs to be done."
- **The models redesigned** (direction): the trousers continued under the coat so the legs read joined to the body;
  the waist less extreme; all three ready-made miners made much prettier and natural, following the aesthetic guides;
  everything unnatural in all the models looked for. How far it goes (the visual model only, or the rig and the body
  too) he leaves open. The final hi-fi prototype keeps the three miners as ready-made suggestions, and a creator of
  one's own from the prototype's options: "not too many, but some".
- **A raised leg's front flap rests on something, and the hem's point is rounded** (decided). Whether only the
  switch-on version changes he left to judgment.
- **Faults he sees:** the leg sometimes clips straight through the skirt; the getting up still shows unnatural leg
  movements.
- "Smaller scale, not smaller quality."
