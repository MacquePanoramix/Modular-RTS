# Arms as strong as its body needs — October 14, 2026

**From:** Luis (Game Director), answering the question of October 14
about the miners' arms
([the question](../Design/TheBodysOwn.md#a-question-for-luis-its-arms)).

## What was asked

Small cannot push its chest off the ground with its arms. An arm's
strength was reckoned from its thickness against an ordinary grown
person's: Small's give about a third of that, and its head is a quarter
of its weight. So a miner cannot get up through its hands and knees,
the plainest way a person does. Its legs had been made "as strong as its
own body needs" on October 7. Should its arms be too? Two answers were
laid out: **A**, arms as strong as its own body needs (getting up
through hands and knees becomes possible; its work with the pickaxe gets
stronger too, and has to be measured again); **B**, arms as they are.
I leaned to A.

## The message (verbatim)

> Okay yeah I think we can go with A and adapt the rest to it as necessary. Please proceed.

## What it says

| | Luis | What follows |
|---|---|---|
| The arms | "we can go with A" | **Decided by Luis:** a miner's arms are as strong as its own body needs, as its legs are |
| Its work | "adapt the rest to it as necessary" | What the stronger arms change in the work of S3 (the swing, carrying, tiring) is measured again, and put right where it no longer reads right. What "right" is stays Luis's to judge |
| What next | "Please proceed." | The getting up goes on, through hands and knees |

## The second message, right after (verbatim)

> Also please before doing the changes, do a really in depth reference research into  how people are animated getting up

And a third, right after that (verbatim):

> Also not just from games but also just understand from even other medias like anime or animation or anything to really understand what looks natural in general.

So before anything is changed (the arms, the getting up), a deep
reference research on how people get up and how getting up is
animated, in games and in every other medium (animation, anime, film,
stage and dance, sport, children), for what reads as natural in
general:
[Research, October 14](../Research/2026-10-14_HowPeopleGetUp.md).

## What Luis did not say

- How strong "as its own body needs" is, in figures. That is set by a
  rule written down beside the legs' rule, and measured.
- Whether the work should look exactly as before, or may change with
  the arms.
