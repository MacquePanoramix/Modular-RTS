# The essence beyond the surface — October 2, 2026

**From:** Luis (Game Director), after seeing the hand-painted pass (S1c,
second pass).
**Attachment:** seven reference images, sent as a WhatsApp zip. They are
frames and fan works around Studio Ghibli films, so they are **not
committed** (copyright). They are described below so the record stays
useful without them.

> okay here's my feeling about it.
>

Now, we could just leave it as is, but I'd like us explore even further beyond to see if we can capture not just the visual, but the breath-taking out of this world emotion that I seek to feel and can't yet feel quite yet. ## The seven images (described, not committed)

1. **The moving castle in the highlands** (Howl's Moving Castle background
   art). A huge mechanical castle stands on a flowered slope. Beyond it a
   sunlit green valley falls to a turquoise lake, below a misty mountain with
   a snow peak. Storm-grey and white clouds tower over a cerulean sky. A
   shaft of light lands on the valley floor.
2. **The cottage among flower rivers** (a fan painting in Ghibli's manner).
   A small stone cottage with a mossy roof stands on a meadow cut by winding
   blue streams. Lupines in lilac and pink, and orange and yellow flowers,
   fill the foreground. Two tiny figures stand on a little bridge. Enormous
   cream-and-lilac cumulus clouds rise between grey-blue mountains.
3. **The lakeside picnic** (Howl's Moving Castle). An old woman and a boy at
   a table on a grassy shore. A turquoise lake, a dark conifer forest and pale
   blue far hills under a peach-to-blue sky.
4. **Six town and garden frames** (Howl's Moving Castle). Saturated
   half-timbered streets at night with a lit lamp, a pink-and-gold palace
   under a bright sky with flags, a violet storm sky over a bridge, a red
   tiled shed swallowed by pink flowers, and a statue square.
5. **The sea railway, three frames** (Spirited Away). A lone house and tree
   on a green islet in a mirror-still sea, under towering clouds. The sea
   train crosses the water at sunset below apricot and mauve cloud banks. The
   rails run under shallow water into a lilac dusk.
6. **The sea railway, six frames** (Spirited Away). Clear noon blue with the
   red-and-cream train on turquoise water, the islet house, a pale golden haze
   over the water, the sunset train, the lilac dusk and a deep blue night
   with one small light.
7. **Spirited Away collage.** The bathhouse at a purple starry dusk with
   lanterns, Chihiro among pink flowers, No-Face under a lit lantern, and a
   red lantern-lit gate.

## How it was recorded

- **The verdict.** The hand-painted pass is "already quite beautiful" and
  matches much of his taste, but it is not yet the emotion he seeks. He
  wants to look "even further beyond" rather than stop there.
- **The aim.** Not just the visual, but a breathtaking, out-of-this-world
  emotion, "the image from my mind's eye straight out of a dream".
- **The method he asks for.** Seek the stylistic and artistic essence, not
  the surface. Time and research are welcome: "I don't mind we spend a long
  time in this checkpoint".
- **What follows.** The research and the essence it found are in
  [TheEssence.md](../ArtDirection/TheEssence.md), and the exploration it
  started is S1e in [NextMilestonePlan.md](../NextMilestonePlan.md).
- **Every frame a painting.** The rendering style, not only the environment,
  must make any frame the camera lands on beautiful and driven by light. The
  game must still run well as a modular RTS. In answer, the rendering language
  became the main track of S1e. Look F's painting pass and palette grade came
  first; frame cost is measured at every step.
- **Unique, not similar.** The Visual Soul is the artistic soul. The aim is
  something above and beyond it, unmistakably Wonder Gather's own, not a
  resemblance to its references or to Ghibli. See "Wonder Gather's own
  language" in [TheEssence.md](../ArtDirection/TheEssence.md).
- **The verdict.** Luis prefers the hand-painted pass ("the before") to S1e's
  first iteration.
  - The hand-painted pass stays the base. S1e's first iteration is not
    adopted.
  - Its code is kept on the archive branch `claude/essence-exploration`, and
    its record in [TheEssencePlaytest.md](../Playtests/TheEssencePlaytest.md).
  - What his favourite frame says about his taste is read in
    [TheEssence.md](../ArtDirection/TheEssence.md#luiss-verdict-and-what-his-favourite-frame-teaches).
- **The merge.** He did not ask for the hand-painted pass to be merged, so it
  stays on the branch.
