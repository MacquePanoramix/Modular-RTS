# Luis says yes to the ankle mend's build, and turns Small's learnt addition off in the game (November 15, 2026, afternoon)

The ankle mend was researched, revised against a critic and checked by a second one
([the research](../Research/2026-11-15_NaturalRangesAndTheAnkle/research.md)). Claude then sent Luis the proposal in
plain words, with two questions. His reply, kept as received:

> About the questions:
>
> 1. Sure.
> 2. Yes option a sounds good.

## What he was asked

1. "May I build the three switches, off by default, and run those tests? You'd see the results before anything is
   turned on." The switches:
   - **A**, a limit for the get-up's ankle that cannot jump across. Setting 1 keeps today's reach. Setting 2 is the
     real human ankle range, from published measurements, set a little inside the average; it changes poses, so it
     goes to the aesthetic judges first, then to him.
   - **B**, a speed limit for the boot, which brakes as it reaches its target. Bigger bodies get a lower limit; how
     much lower is still open.
   - **C**, each boot placed from its own shin when the body falls. It comes last, never on its own.
   - A fourth, a brake stronger than the push, would change the body's strength. It stays his to decide, and only if
     the three are not enough.
   - The tests: the worst cases forced first (a free boot turned half a circle, a pure twist, a fall while a boot
     spins); then B alone, A alone, both, then C, each over 24 courses on Small, Long and Round, read frame by frame.
2. "Small's learnt helper." Small alone adds nudges of up to 20 degrees to its boots while it gets up, from the
   October training. They come after the ankle's limit, so they can push past it, and the last one is never cleared.
   - **(a)** Off in the game, kept only for training, so every body gets up by the same rules. This was recommended:
     "one body for everything", "any body", and his "goofy" of October 30. He was told that Small's get-up would
     change a little.
   - **(b)** Kept, but added before the limit and cleared when its stages end.

He was also told that the idle stays as it is. The switches live only in the get-up's part of the body, which does not
exist while the body stands, and the standing settings are put back the moment it is up. This is to be proved tick by
tick before he looks.

## What it decides

- The three switches are built, off by default, and tested in the order proposed (research, section 7.2). None is
  turned on by default without his word. Setting 2 of A, and the asks formed in joint space, go to aesthetic judges
  first, then to him. The fourth, the brake, stays **Open**, his.
- Small's learnt addition is off as played (`RiseAgent.Unheard` true by default) and kept for training. The live test
  is taught to read `-ownUnheard`, so that both ways can be measured.
- Small's get-up as played changes a little. Its baseline is run both ways once, to show him what turning the addition
  off changes; the mend's arms then run as played, with it off.
