# The worker concepts — Luis's feedback — October 2, 2026

**From:** Luis (Game Director), after seeing the first worker concepts (Round,
Long, Small) in the Ordinary Place
([WorkerConcepts.md](../ArtDirection/WorkerConcepts.md)).

## Message (verbatim)

> From these, my order of liking of the whole character as a whole in order were the small, then the long then the round. The face I loved of each one. Actually for all of them I liked the idea of the character a lot, but I feel all of them can be much more pretty and aesthetically pleasing to look at in general. Like the model quality can be much better still I feel. I like how styalized they are though. Maybe we can even have in the final prototype the choice to chose between these 3 characters. Reminder that they are all supposed to represent this miner. I like that they are all super distinct and unique and not generic and with soul. Just make their model higher quality also and maybe give some hints even if small related to them being miners. Anyways also keep in mind this is supposed to relate to the final game where people will customize everything fully in a modular way, including the units, down to appareance also like in those super detailed character creators in video games.

## How it was recorded

- **Luis's ranking, as whole characters:**
  1. Small;
  2. Long;
  3. Round.
- **What Luis loved:** the face of each one, and the idea of every character.
  They are distinct, unique, not generic, and have soul.
- **The stylization:** Luis likes how stylized they are, so it stays.
- **What to change.** All three should be much prettier and more pleasing to
  look at, with much higher model quality.
- **They are all the miner.** Give each some hints of mining, even small ones.
- **A possible choice of characters.** "Maybe" the final prototype lets the
  player choose between the three. This is recorded as a possibility, not a
  decision.
- **Modular, all the way down.** The final game lets players customize
  everything, units included, down to appearance, like detailed character
  creators. The models are built from modules (body, head and face, hair,
  garments, accessories) so they can grow into that creator.
