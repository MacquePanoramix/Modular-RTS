## How it was recorded

- **Small, to fix:**
  - **The leg** still does not line up with the boot.
  - **The laces' crosses** float in the air, not on the boot.
  - **The satchel's strap** must join the bag.
  - **The lantern** looks held from far away, but floating up close.
  - **The bag should hang with gravity** ("the back" in the message, read as
    the bag, the object being discussed), not float.
- **A principle restated.** Physicality and the reality of all movement are
  among the game's main focuses. Anything that reads as an object should
  behave as a proper object that responds to its environment, never float.
- **Liked:** how the coat's skirt, the lantern and the bag move when the
  character walks.
- **A method first.** Before these fixes, write a strategy, to keep and refer
  to, for making the models as polished as possible:
  - look at every object and every place from all angles;
  - search each angle exhaustively for anything weird or worth polishing;
  - research how to reach the highest polish;
  - develop a method for raising model quality from now on.

  The method is [ModelQualityMethod.md](../ArtDirection/ModelQualityMethod.md).
