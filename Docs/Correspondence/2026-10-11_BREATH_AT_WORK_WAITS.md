# Breath on a working miner waits for steps 4 and 6 — October 11, 2026

**From:** Luis (Game Director), in answer to one question
([the plan, B15](../NextMilestonePlan.md#questions-for-luis)).

## The question, as it was put

> Should a miner resting from its blows heave now (about a day on the
> old body), or wait until walking and work become the body's own in
> steps 4 and 6? I'd wait.

It came with the report on the pickaxe miss
([the design page](../Design/TheBodysOwn.md#the-miss-at-a-low-boulder-october-10)):
breath drawn on a working miner's chest and shoulders moves where its
blows land, so it is drawn there only on a miner standing by its own
joints with empty hands, or down.

## The message (verbatim)

> Sure we can wait first for steps 4 and 6.

## What it says

| | Luis | What follows |
|---|---|---|
| Breath on the chest and shoulders of a miner at work, or resting from it | "Sure we can wait first for steps 4 and 6." | **Decided: it waits.** Nothing is done for it on the body as it was. It is to come with walking (step 4) and the work (step 6) as each becomes the body's own, where a breath is asked of the joints themselves. Until then such a miner shows its breath only in the air, from dusk to dawn |

## What Luis did not say

- Anything of the build, of the three faults, or of what I had got
  wrong: the report asked nothing of them. **The mending of the miss is
  not accepted by this**, and whether the build was opened is not said.
- That the order of the steps changes. It does not: the step (step 3)
  is next.
