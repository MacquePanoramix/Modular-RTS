# The direction for any body — November 3, 2026

**From:** Luis (Game Director), answering the six questions of the proposal of October 30
([Design/GettingUpForAnyBody.md](../Design/GettingUpForAnyBody.md)): (1) the direction, a body working out its own way up
from the stances people use, built from a body that cannot look broken; (2) whether the stances, written once as goals for
any body, count as "pre-made"; (3) bodies that cannot get up alone; (4) how long getting up may take; (5) the references
(the app's window open for the frame-by-frame study; the CMU motion capture downloaded as reference); (6) Small's present
way, and the old way after 12 tries, kept until the new way stands reliably.

## The message (verbatim)

> 1. Yeah we can go with this direction I think, seems the most prudent.
> 2. I think so? I guess I would have to check if it looks to robotic or repetitive, but based on what you said I think should be fine like that. You can keep an eye on it though.
> 3. I think it should give a warning in those cases in the unit creator, but still allow it to be built and then we can/should later implement an option for using a unit to try to help another unit to get up from the ground (also following the same principles as getting up by it's own with no pre-made animations, so you got a good instinct on it well done :)
> 4. I mean yeah, try to just follow something that looks natural in general. Depending on the person if it can make sense that this kind of person would take a very very long time to get up it's very okay. It doesn't even need to be fully realistic, but at least make some amount of natural sence even if from a media perspective.
> 5. Yes please.
> 6. Okay yeah that makes sense.

## What it says

| | Luis | What follows |
|---|---|---|
| 1. The direction | "we can go with this direction I think, seems the most prudent" | **Decided by Luis:** the capacity-driven procedural getting up, built in layers from a body that cannot look broken (the proposal's layers 0 to 4; a learned correction only later and only if needed) |
| 2. The stances | "I think so? ... I would have to check if it looks to robotic or repetitive ... You can keep an eye on it though" | **Decided, provisionally:** the stances people pass through, written once as goals relative to any body, are allowed; the movement between them is the body's own. To be watched, and shown to him, for anything robotic or repetitive |
| 3. Bodies that cannot get up alone | "a warning in those cases in the unit creator, but still allow it to be built"; later "an option for using a unit to try to help another unit to get up from the ground", by "the same principles ... with no pre-made animations" | **Decided by Luis:** such bodies may be made, with a warning in the creator. **Direction, later:** one unit helping another up, physically, by the same principles. Neither is built now (the creator is S2) |
| 4. How long | "follow something that looks natural in general"; "if it can make sense that this kind of person would take a very very long time ... it's very okay"; "doesn't even need to be fully realistic, but at least make some amount of natural sence even if from a media perspective" | **Direction:** time to stand follows the body's capacity; a very limited body may take a very long time; natural in the sense of reading as natural (as in film and animation), not measured to the second |
| 5. References | "Yes please." | **Approved:** the CMU motion capture's get-ups downloaded as reference (never played); the frame-by-frame study goes on whenever the app's window is open |
| 6. The present way | "Okay yeah that makes sense." | **Decided:** Small's present way, and the old way after 12 tries, stay until the new way stands reliably |
