## The questions, as they were put

1. **The "centimetre or two" measure.** The plan says a standing
   miner's head wanders no more than that. Between weight shifts it
   keeps to millimetres; across a shift its head goes 3 to 4 cm. I
   think the measure should apply only between shifts.
2. **How breath is shown.** Drawn on the chest it is not apparent. I
   would next try the shoulders rising and breath visible in the dusk
   air, beside the three looks on the panel.
3. **Step before knees?** I suggest doing the step (plan step 3) before
   getting from knees onto feet (step 2), since leaning, a real weight
   shift and standing up to a push all wait on it.
