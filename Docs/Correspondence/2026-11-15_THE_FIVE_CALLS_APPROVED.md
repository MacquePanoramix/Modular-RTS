## What he was shown

- **The blink:** the eyes blink when the body has a reason: a big turn of the head, a hard effort, a landing. At rest
  they blink at an uneven pace, slower when the miner concentrates and quicker when tired. Never a steady tick.
- **Folds in the clothes:** when a miner bends deeply, the coat bunches. The folds are worked out by simulating the
  same coat at real poses, never sculpted by hand. The flaps keep swinging as now, and it must work for any body the
  creator makes.
- **Knees and elbows:** today the skin pinches thin like a bent hose when a joint bends far. These fixes make the skin
  keep its volume as flesh does. They move only when the joints move, and they are zero at rest.
- **Small body shapes:** the point of the elbow, the calf and the kneecap, drawn on the surface only. The physics body
  underneath keeps its size and weight.
- **Slits in the coat's hem:** shown in pictures beside today's coat, for him to pick. They might also help with the
  leg poking through the skirt.

Each is an option behind a switch, off by default, and with any of them on the idle standing moves exactly as it does
now.

## What it decides

The five calls are now his, as written in the vision's row of November 15. They are rulings on what counts as the
body's own and as physical; they are not looks. Every look still goes to aesthetic judges first and then to him in
pictures, and the coat's vents stay **Open** as a costume until he picks.

The separate reviewer planned for the calls before he saw them was not run, since he approved them directly. Its two
open questions go to the judges who check each item when it is built:
- whether the uneven rest blinks read as true to real bodies, never as a timer;
- whether garment shapes made once on the shared template carry to every body the creator can make.
