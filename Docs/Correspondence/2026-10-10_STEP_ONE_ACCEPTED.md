## One thing I told Luis that was half wrong

In the report these questions came with I wrote of the miss: "The swing
is planned from where the shoulders are drawn, and something in it is
sensitive to a centimetre. That fault is older than breath." That was a
guess. The older fault has nothing to do with shoulders: the miner
itself is taken off its place by its own steps, and swung from where
it stood. With breath drawn, the blows do move, as I said; that it is
by its arms being lifted is still only what I take to be the cause
([the design page](../Design/TheBodysOwn.md#the-miss-at-a-low-boulder-october-10)).

## One thing I did that was wrong

The switch went on from the start, and I copied a build for Luis,
before the mining test had once been run with it on: every test but
those of the standing body itself was pinned to the body as it was. A
reviewer asked for it. Run as the game is played, the test failed three
times of three (an older fault: a blow shared between a rock and a bush
went to the bush). It is mended, the test can now be run as played, and
the build was made again. Nothing had been told to Luis of the first
build.
