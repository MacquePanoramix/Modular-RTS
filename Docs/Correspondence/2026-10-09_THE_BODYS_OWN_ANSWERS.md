# The body's own: Luis's four answers — October 9, 2026 (second message)

**From:** Luis (Game Director), in answer to the report at the end of
[the round of October 9](../Reviews/2026-10-09_TheBodysOwn_Round.md),
which put four questions (B1 to B4 of the proposal for the body's own,
now [the plan in force](../NextMilestonePlan.md)).

## The questions, as they were put

1. Is "no active ragdoll" (September 30) replaced by your direction of
   October 9? I read it so.
2. Does this come before S4? I recommend yes, because carrying cannot be
   the body's own on a body that walks posed.
3. Are my measures of "stable" right? They are in the plan: the same
   pulls it stands through today, and no fall in ten minutes of walking.
4. May breathing be drawn on the chest, with its rate taken from how hard
   the body has worked? A chest cannot be pushed from inside by a joint.

## The message (verbatim)

> 1. Yes.
> 2. Yeah I also think so, we will probably end up doing a big rework I imagine for it.
> 3. Yeah they looked pretty good for me.
> 4. If you think it will look nice/apparent, then sure. If not then we can think of other ways to represent it. But yeah breathing is good to have while following the visual aesthetic of the game for it too.
