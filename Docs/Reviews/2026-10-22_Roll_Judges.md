The first round for a new movement: Small, lying on its back, rolls onto its
front by its own limbs, to get up from there (Luis, October 22: "never the
backup animation"; [the message](../Correspondence/2026-10-22_NEVER_THE_BACKUP_ANIMATION.md)).
Three new judges (the body, the animator, the sceptic), separate agents on the
smaller model, each with its brief and `alive.md` (`Art/Review/judges/`), two
sheets, what it was told to do, and figures read from the trace. Not told what
had been changed, what I think, or anything before.

- **The tries:** work branch, `ways_roll_small.txt` (`Docs/Technical/RiseWays/`
  on the work branch), Small from its back (`back@1`) and after being pushed

- **The sheets** ([folder](../Images/GettingUp_2026-10-22/judges_roll/)): each
  roll, 0.2 s apart, from 0 to 2.4 s.
- **What it was told:** lying on its back, roll over onto its front by its own
  limbs (a knee coming up and across, an arm reaching over), and lie on its
  front ready to push itself up.
- **Figures given:** 40.7 kg; shoulders 42 N m, elbows 36; the knee up and arm
  across 0 to 0.6 s, the push over 0.6 to about 1.4 s, settling from there; on
  its front and still at 2.58 s and 2.50 s; its trunk and head at most 1.7 and
  1.2 m/s.

**All three: "reads as real, with a flaw."** The gather is real (a knee up, its
foot planted, a hand up, stable on its back; the body could be frozen in any of
those frames); a leg swung over is a real way to roll; its parts do not move on
one clock (the knee, then the arm, the head before the trunk, the head landing
last); what hangs on it swings; nothing slides, pops or passes through the
ground at this size; about two seconds to its front, a little quicker than a
person, right for this character.
