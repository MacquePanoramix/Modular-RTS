## What stands, and what was done

1. **Long's and Round's arms like wings in the deep flat squat** (all three judges). The cause: the shoulders lifted
   sideways about 100 degrees, because an arm hanging along a trunk leant 60 degrees forward would go into the ground, and
   the pull left the sideways lift free. **Mended** (`77cc4ec`): the pull takes the sideways lift toward 10 degrees, and
   the relaxing pass also starts from the fit with its free arms reached forward; now forward, 50 to 54 degrees, out 10 to
   18. [The sheets since](../../Images/StanceAtlas/2026-11-04/). Not judged again yet.
2. **The squat on the toes as fitted** (all three): the relaxed look is the one kept.
3. **Long's bear crawl (hands and feet, D 0.50) folds into a pike**, the hips high, the limbs parallel stilts (fit 0.42).
   A fault of the stance for a tall body, not of the arms: open.
4. **Sitting up on the hands behind**: Small and Round on straight, locked arms with the head thrown back; their reserve
   is high (S 0.56 and 0.40): a stance these two would rather not take, which the choice of way (layer 2) is to weigh.
5. **Round's forearm "held up near the chin"** in the stand and kneels: the solids' doing. Round's shoulders sit right
   under its big head sphere and its arm hangs over the bulging hip capsule (the arm's angles: hanging, 3 degrees forward,
   a 23 degree elbow). To be read again on the drawn miner.
6. **The rear leg ruler-straight rising from the half-kneel** (Small, Long): the stride the stance asks; open.
7. **Repetition and stiffness**: the same few arm places recur, and the stands and kneels are mirror-perfect. The
   strategy's seeded variation (layer 2) and the asymmetry of a living body are for later; noted, as Luis asked.
8. **The solids themselves** (the wide hip capsule swallowing hands and thighs, the head sphere without a neck, the box
   boots, rods without hands): not of the posture; the drawn miners come next.

Their verdicts are not Luis's.
