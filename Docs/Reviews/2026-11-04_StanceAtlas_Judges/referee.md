# Referee: nine stances, A vs B, Small / Long / Round

All nine sheets and the three body pages were read at full size.

## Findings that stand (most serious first)
1. Squat 0.35 flat, Long A/B (fit 0.49/0.48) and Round A/B (0.34). The arms stick out sideways and up like wings, or one is raised beside the head. All three judges agree. SEEN, plainly, in the front and back views. A and B are equally bad. Long A is the widest. Action: the free arms need a rule (hang, or reach forward for balance).
2. Squat 0.40 toes, version A in Small, Long and Round. Both forearms are stacked forward at head height, or the hands are at the face. SEEN in the side views of Small A and Round A, and in Long A, where the hands are at the face. The same shape on three builds reads as given. The centre of mass is only +12 to +16 mm inside the base, so reaching forward is justified here, but not at the face. B (arms down along the shins) reads better in all three bodies. Fit is nearly equal (A 0.16/0.16/0.19, B 0.18/0.18/0.21).
3. Bear crawl D=0.50, Long A/B (fit 0.42). SEEN. The trunk is folded, the head hangs lower than the hips, the hips are small under the trunk, and the limbs are parallel stilts. It does not read as a bear crawl. A and B are identical. Small and Round are acceptable.
4. Sit-up, hands behind, Small (S 0.56) and Round (S 0.40). SEEN. The arms are straight props and the head is thrown back. Long (S 0.15) is believable. A and B are identical.
5. Round: one forearm is held up in front of the chest or chin. SEEN in the side views of stand, tall kneel, half-kneel and rising, in both A and B. Only the body judge named it in full; the animator and sceptic saw part of it. It is odd for a plain stand.
6. Small B squat flat: a forearm across the eyes. SEEN (front and left views). Small A is better.
7. Rising from the half-kneel: the rear leg is a ruler-straight line from hip to toe in Small and Long. SEEN. The stride is long.
8. Repetition across stances. SEEN. The same arm slots recur: forearm across the belly or thigh, hand at the face, arms straight forward. The legs, trunk and plumb are the same in A and B in most stances. A and B differ almost only in the arms.
9. Symmetry in the front views (stand, tall kneel, hands and knees, bear). SEEN. It is mannequin-like, but it is expected for a solved stance.

## Struck or downgraded
- Sceptic: "plumb far in front of the feet" in the Long bear crawl. NOT THERE. The plumb falls between hands and feet (+195 mm).
- Sceptic: the sit-up hip solid "floats above the legs". NOT SEEN. It rests on the ground, and the tint marks load.
- Body: "Round B arm wrapped around the head" in the squat toes. NOT THERE. In the front view the arm hangs beside the head and down.
- Body: Long A rising, trunk tilted in the back view. UNCLEAR. The tilt is slight, and the back view is foreshortened.
- Body and sceptic: Small half-kneel, the front foot turned in and the back toes at an odd angle. UNCLEAR. It is a view effect of the toe boxes.
- Animator: "knees dead straight" in the stand. Mostly not there. A slight knee bend shows in the side views (Long, Small), which is the sceptic's reading. Minor.
- Arms piercing the hip capsule in the stand and half-kneel side views. SEEN, but it is a property of the solids; the finished character may hide it.
- Hip capsule hiding the thighs, rod arms with no hands, boot boxes standing like planks, the head sphere with no gaze. All SEEN, all caused by the solids, not by the posture.

## Disagreements
- Rising, A or B. The animator slightly prefers A (arms hang, but limp). The body judge and the sceptic slightly prefer B (a forearm across the belly looks like a guard, but also "held"). The frames show only this difference in the arms. Fit is 0.16 and 0.17 in Small, and equal in Long and Round. This is arm taste, so: no difference.
- Half-kneel, Long. The body judge prefers A (hand on the knee). The frames show B with the left shoulder loaded and the arm swung out. A is slightly better.

## Verdict per stance (A, B or no difference)
- Stand: no difference (all three).
- Hands and knees: no difference.
- Bear crawl: no difference (Long is poor in both).
- Squat flat: Small A (B has a hand over the eyes). Long: no difference, both poor. Round: no difference, both poor.
- Squat toes: B in all three. Long B has splayed legs in the front view, but its arms are better.
- Tall kneel: no difference.
- Half-kneel: Long A. Small and Round: no difference.
- Rising: no difference (arm taste only).
- Sit-up: no difference.

Not seen by any judge: the legs of the flat squat are hidden by the hip solid, so their angle cannot be judged. Alive qualities (breath, timing, arcs) cannot be judged from stills.

**These stand:** 1 to 9 above, in that order.
