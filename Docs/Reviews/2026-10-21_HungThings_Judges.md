## What stands

The ground: what hangs lies on it and is held there; nothing in it but a few
millimetres. Lain on: the lantern and the mug go out along the ground (Round's
hammer lain on is still hidden in its torso). The thigh: the hammer no longer goes
through it on hands and knees; in a deep squat its upper handle still sinks into the
thigh for about 0.1 s, and its kick out is judged too quick by two judges of three.
Older and open: the mug cut by the coat as Long falls; the lantern held out at
rest; things that settle without a swing back; a one-look flick of the stop's
fallback. None of this is Luis's verdict.
