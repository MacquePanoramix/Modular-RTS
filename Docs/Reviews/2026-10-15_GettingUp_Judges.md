## Their eight things (alive), together

Never quite still: no in the holds. Not on one clock: yes (hips then head;
toes, then knee, then foot). Gets ready: in part (the hump before the push, the
toes tipping before the foot; no lean before the lift). What hangs goes on: in
part (satchel and hair while it moves; nothing settles after the foot is
down). Arcs: in part (the foot's path is hidden). Does not repeat: cannot be
seen (one try). Balance by degrees: in part (it dips and is caught by the foot;
no give at elbows or ankles). Effort and tiredness: in part (the push reads as
labour; no tremble, no tiredness).
