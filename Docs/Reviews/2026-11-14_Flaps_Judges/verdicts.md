better in 5 as well (the old way's fin, crushed bag and floating trouser end).

## What it means, and what is left

The switch takes away the detached leg wherever the leg is below square, and for the leg left lying in every pose.
Left with it on: the pointed front hem; a flap standing over a leg raised past square (Drape has no belly to stop the
front flap); and, from the feet, what the skirt's opening shows: the trousers' cut tops, the art's cut (Small and
Round; `Art/Blender/Worker/workers.py`, `hidden_above`). The guide judge read the cut against
`CharacterPractices.md`'s own condition: geometry is removed where no one can see it, and lying down, it is seen.
