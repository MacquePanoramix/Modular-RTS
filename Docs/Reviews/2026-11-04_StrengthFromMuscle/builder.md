# Builder review: what will bite later

Read: README, both diffs, PhysicalBody.cs, MinerSetup.cs (around Weigh and SetMuscleShares), PhysicalBodyTests.cs, TestArguments.cs, results/. Not checked: any game code outside these files (PhysicalHands, the creator, the scenes, other prefabs, ProceduralBiped.SavedProportions), the real prefabs, and nothing was run (no Unity). Arithmetic: the quoted capacity pairs differ by 0.26 to 0.8 percent, as claimed.

## Ranked findings

1. **One field has two meanings (sure it is a design problem; how much it bites is a question).** `armsMuscle` and `legsMuscle` are at once the player-facing strength stat ("1 = an ordinary body of its size", PhysicalBody.cs 124-128) and a per-body calibration constant that cancels the miners' drawn limb thickness (SharesAsNeeded, 134-150). The creator will show Long as 1.40 arms and 1.47 legs and Round as 0.81 and 0.80. They are not muscle, they are "how wrong the drawn radius was". Consequences:
   - A creator that lets the player "reset to ordinary" (1.0) on Long gives it a knee of 137 N m instead of 202. The README says this exact body could not rise (10 failures).
   - When strength is later shown on the body (thickness or leanness from the share), Long reads thin but 40 percent over-muscled, and Round thick but "0.8". Share and radius are also double counted: capacity is share x radius^3, so a creator that changes the radius and the share separately gets a product nobody chose.
   - Fix to consider now, while it is cheap: keep a separate per-body baseline (calibration, written by MinerSetup) and a stat that multiplies it, with 1 meaning "as built". Or at least rename and document it.
2. **The shares are a frozen snapshot of the Need rule on Nov 4 (sure).** `SharesAsNeeded` reads ArmHolds, KneeHolds, `SavedProportions.legSegment` and the mass as they are today. If any of those constants or the weights are retuned, the prefabs do not follow, while `-strengthRule need` does, so claims 1 and 3 stop agreeing silently. If Rules.Need is ever deleted (likely once Muscle is final), the calibration cannot be regenerated, and MinerSetup.SetMuscleShares and SharesAsNeeded stop compiling. Bake the numbers as data, or write down the order of removal. A test is missing: "each miner's shoulder, elbow and knee under Muscle are within 1 percent of the same under Need". It would catch every case in this list. Today only the two eased swing tests would notice, and by luck.
3. **Re-running MinerSetup or changing proportions (sure for the first, likely for the second).** MinerSetup.Weigh (507-511) calls `Configure` then `SharesAsNeeded`, so a remade miner recalibrates. That is right. But it also silently overwrites any share a person set in the prefab, because SharesAsNeeded starts with `armsMuscle = legsMuscle = 1` and then replaces them. Back and neck are not touched. Two cases are not covered:
   - Proportions changed at runtime (a creator rescaling armRadius, legRadius, or the proportions): the shares keep compensating the old radius. The knee need is computed from `SavedProportions.legSegment` but the Need branch of KneeCapacity (251-255) uses `BodyProportions.legSegment`. They agree only while the two are equal.
   - `Configure` does not reset the cached `shoulderNeeds` and `elbowNeeds` (ArmNeeds, 225), so calling Configure again on the same instance uses stale arm lengths. Reset them in Configure.
4. **The static Rule leaks and is flipped by non-test code (sure for the mechanism; no failing case seen).**
   - `SharesAsNeeded` (140-143) and `SetMuscleShares` (531-534) switch the global `PhysicalBody.Rule` to Need and back, with no try/finally. If `ShoulderCapacity` or `ArmNeeds` throws (destroyed bone, `parts` null), the whole editor domain is left on Need until the next reload. `SharesAsNeeded` is public and could be called at run time (a creator), flipping a global while other bodies read it. Better to compute the Need numbers through explicit functions that take the rule as a parameter, not by flipping a static.
   - `TestArguments.End` restores a hard-coded Muscle, not what it found. With domain reload off, or a second SetUpFixture, that is a second copy of the default. `ArmsAsBodyNeeds` is the same kind of public mutable static and nothing resets it. I found no test setting either (grep), so this is future risk only.
   - The SetUpFixture sets the rule only for the WonderGather.Tests namespace. Tests elsewhere (EditMode, other assemblies) now run on Muscle by default, where they used to run on Need. Whether any of them build bodies with Configure and no shares I cannot see (a question).
5. **Bodies other than the three miners (a question).** The default flipped for every PhysicalBody. Any body made by Configure without SharesAsNeeded (workers on this branch, test-built bodies, scene or prefab copies serialized before the fields existed, which get the initializer value 1) changed from Need to uncalibrated Muscle silently. The README speaks of three miners only; please confirm there are no others. A grep for `Configure(` callers outside MinerSetup would settle it, and I could not do it.
6. **The share does not mean one thing across regions (sure).** For arms and legs it multiplies a radius^3 capacity. For back and neck it multiplies the body-need value (BackCapacity 191, the neck lines in OwnBody 635 and PhysicalFall 410), and only under Muscle (`MuscleGives`). So a heavy thin-limbed body gets weak arms and knees (cubed) but a back and neck that grow with its weight, so "heavy body on thin limbs is weaker" is only half true and a back share of 1 is not "ordinary for its size" in the same sense. Also: WristCapacity and HoldCapacity are not scaled by the arms share at all (243-246), so arms 2.0 doubles shoulder and elbow but not the grip or wrist. That will look wrong once strength is shown on the body. Strength (0.1-4) times share (0.1-4) is a double dial; decide which one the creator exposes.
7. **No validation on the serialized shares (likely).** `SetMuscle` clamps to 0.1-4, but the four `[SerializeField]` fields are not clamped (no OnValidate, no Range), and `SharesAsNeeded` clamps silently (a body that needs more than 4x ends up weaker, with no log). A zero or negative hand-edited share gives a zero capacity; divisions such as PhysicalHands.cs 691 (`/ physical.ShoulderOf(i)`) are not visibly guarded in the lines I saw (PhysicalBalance guards with Max(1e-3)). Log when a clamp happens in SharesAsNeeded.
8. **Serialization and names.**
   - The fields `armsMuscle` etc. have no `FormerlySerializedAs`; renaming them for the creator (e.g. to a Strength-style name) silently resets all three miners to 1, which is exactly the broken state (Long cannot rise). Add the attribute or a test that reads the miners' prefab shares.
   - `MuscleOf` (raw stored) and `MuscleGives` (what it does now) differ by one word and the same call site can use either. A UI calling `MuscleOf` shows a number that does nothing under Need. ShoulderCapacity and KneeCapacity read the fields directly while Back and the neck go through `MuscleGives`: two gating paths. Use one. Suggest `MuscleShare` (stored) and `EffectiveShare` or fold the rule check into one accessor.
   - Prefab diffs also add `StaysPut: 0` to three other components, so "written into their prefabs as they are, nothing else touched" is not quite true: the re-save reserialized other components. Harmless as shown, but it says the prefabs were stale relative to the code; look for whether other fields were added anywhere else (the diff shows only this one).
9. **Tests are on a knife edge (sure).** The eased lift check is `> ordinary*1.2 - .01` (0.72 > 0.71) and the feeble lift is 0.72 or 0.74 on identical reruns, so the run is not deterministic. One step lower (0.70) fails again. A 0.7 percent change in two arm joints moving a result 61 to 59 percent and 37 to 36 steps shows how sensitive the swing is; any later change (any-proportion bodies, retuned constants) will flip these again, and easing thresholds one at a time hides it. Prefer asserting the monotone ordering (feeble slower than ordinary by at least a full step or a larger margin), or calibrate arms per joint (separate shoulder and elbow shares) so Round matches to under 0.1 percent. 28 tests are skipped (run only when asked); their status under the new default is unknown.
10. **Smaller.** SetMuscleShares sets `SetMuscle(Arms, 1)` (530) that SharesAsNeeded repeats; `shoulderWas` and `elbowWas` read under Need after the share reset are fine because Need ignores the shares now. No try/finally around LoadPrefabContents/Unload (an exception leaks the loaded contents). The `.4f` fallback thigh in SharesAsNeeded when `stance` is null is silent and gives wrong shares; throw or log instead.
