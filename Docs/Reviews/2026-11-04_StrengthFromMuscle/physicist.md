## Findings, most important first

1. (likely) The two failures point at ONE joint, Round's shoulder (+0.71%), not "0.7% of the arms" in general. Mean-of-ratios makes shoulder error = (E/S - 1)/2 and elbow = (S/E - 1)/2 with opposite signs. Round: shoulder +0.71%, elbow -0.67%. Both failures moved the way a STRONGER shoulder moves them: choke 61% -> 59% (less sliding needed) and feeble lift 37 -> 36 steps (faster). A weaker elbow would push choke up, not down. PhysicalSwing.cs:91 and PhysicalHands.cs:675 use the shoulder as the binding joint. So the cause is probably the shoulder alone; README claim 2 ("from that 0.7% on Round's arms") is right but incomplete. Cheap proof: set Round's arms share to its shoulder ratio (shoulder exact) vs. elbow ratio, rerun the two tests. Alternative: separate shoulder and elbow shares, or weight the mean toward the shoulder. Small gets the mirror error (shoulder -0.72%, weaker), which no test happens to catch.
2. (sure, arithmetic) "0.3 to 0.8%" holds: shoulder/elbow %: Small -0.72/+0.85 (log rounding; exact from ratios -0.77/+0.79), Long -0.26/+0.31, Round +0.71/-0.67. Need's shoulder/elbow ratio (1.178, 1.166, 1.144) vs Muscle's fixed 121.3/104.5 = 1.1608 explains it exactly. Nothing hidden.
