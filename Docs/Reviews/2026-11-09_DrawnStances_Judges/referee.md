# Referee: drawn stances (Nov 5)

All twelve pictures read at full size. Seen = I see it too.

## Judges' findings, one by one

### Bear crawl (hands and feet D=0.50)
- Body 1 / Animator 1 / Sceptic 1: head hangs between/below the arms, trunk an arch, hips high. SEEN in all three miners, all four views. Figures: Small fit 0.17, Round 0.22 (not poor), Long 0.40 (near poor). Reserve 0.05-0.08, base +180 to +202 mm: the pose is safe, so the fault is how it reads, not whether it holds.
- Small's head hidden / looks headless: SEEN (front: only hair; back: coat covers the whole upper body). Part of this is the camera and the coat hanging down over the head when inverted.
- Long straight stilt legs and arms, very high hips: SEEN (side views: legs near straight, hips well above shoulders).
- Long's coat covers its head: SEEN in the front view; pick sticks up behind.
- Round: face hidden by hair, white shirt strip showing in front view: SEEN (strip not mentioned by judges, minor).
- Body "hands at the same spot as the head": SEEN in Small and Round front. "Limp not propped": SEEN as impression; the arms are vertical and straight and the neck is slack. No judge could rule on whether the posture asked (hands half a leg ahead) forces an arch; the pictures show an A-frame with the hands only about as far ahead of the feet as the head is long, not a long reach.

### Squat h=0.35 flat
- Long coat flared on the ground (front and back), dark mass between knees: SEEN (front: coat tail spread to the left like a cape; back: coat fanned on the ground behind). Fit 0.49 fits the visible stiffness.
- Hands touch boots, not ground (Sceptic): SEEN in Long left view; hands rest on the boot. Not a fault of balance (base +34 mm).
- Pick far out sideways (Animator): SEEN in Long left/right views; a prop, not a body fault.
- Long "knees at chest": SEEN, but reads plausible in the side views.
- Round apron bunched between knees like a plank, face lost: SEEN (front). Round's left hand hangs wide and out from the leg, not on the ground: SEEN.
- Heels "lifted though flat" on Long: UNCLEAR (boots too small and shaded).
- Small acceptable: SEEN, hands on boots, reads as a crouch.

### Squat h=0.40 toes
- Almost the same picture as the flat squat for all three (Body 3, Sceptic 7): SEEN. Toe lift is not readable at this size; the stance is told apart only by the numbers.
- Near hand floats just off the ground on Small/Long (Animator): UNCLEAR; hand is within a few cm, the camera is high.
- Long front: knees together, torso collapsed forward, tall narrow stack: SEEN.
- Margins +12 to +16 mm "precarious": figures say so (base +12/+16/+13 mm) and the pose looks held, nothing sliding. Small but positive.

### Half-kneel
- Long coat bundled into a black box between knee and foot, mug beside it (Body, Animator, Sceptic): SEEN (front and left); the lead thigh in the left view is clear above it.
- Back foot turned outward in Small/Round back views: PARTLY SEEN (Round back: right foot angled out; Small back: left boot angled). Could be the "toes laid" rule meeting the camera angle; not conclusive.
- Small forward knee "clips into coat" (Body) / grey thigh mass in front (Sceptic): the grey knee sits in front of the coat in the front view; clip UNCLEAR, mass SEEN.
- Round front lead knee out, apron hides leg, arms hang: SEEN. Profiles read fine in all three: SEEN.

### Rising from the half-kneel h=0.70
- Same lunge on all three, arms hanging: SEEN. Upright torso with no lean over the front knee: SEEN. Back leg straight and far behind, reads as a stride: SEEN (side views, strongest on Long). Long front view feet in one line, front foot on its toe: SEEN. Small lantern beside the knee: SEEN, hangs plausibly.
- Disagreement: Animator calls it "the best-read stance", Body and Sceptic call it stiff. Both are true: the silhouette reads as a real lunge; the upper body and arms are inert. The frames bear out the stiffness more than the praise.

### Sitting up, hands behind
- Nearly flat lying: SEEN, but the stance itself says 25 degrees from the ground, so the lowness is asked, not a fault.
- Arms splayed wide like a snow angel (front view, Long, Round, Small): SEEN. This follows from hands placed behind and wide, seen from the front.
- Head thrown back with nothing lifting it: SEEN (all three, strongest on Long and Round side views).
- Legs dead straight and together: SEEN.
- Small reserve 0.56 yet looks relaxed: SEEN in the sense that nothing in the face or arms shows effort; the arms are straight and locked, which is how arm-bearing looks.
- Small back view: head hides the body: SEEN (cannot judge the body there). Long/Round back: hunched heap: SEEN.

### Hands and knees
- Good for all three (Body, Animator): SEEN. Small and Round look weighty; Round's lantern glow under the hand reads well.
- Long front coat splayed in two flaps on the ground, hands small: SEEN. Long head low, neck stretched, like sniffing: SEEN, acceptable.

### Stand, tall kneel
- Read as real: SEEN. Tall kneel (fit 0.00-0.01) is clean on all three, Long best. Weight over the knees, shins laid.
- Long's neck leans forward in stand: SEEN, but it is the character's long nose and neck; not a solver fault.
- Animator "Small tall kneel: legs vanish into the coat": SEEN only in the front view; the other views show the kneel.

### Across stances
- Resting arms straight, identical, no hand on a thigh or knee: SEEN. Note: the brief for this round says unloaded arms are meant to rest, and the free-limbs-rest default was Luis's choice; so this is sameness of the rest, not a bug.
- Heads always bowed or level, same angle per stance: SEEN. Faces blank, no sign of effort even at 0.56: SEEN.
- The three miners differ in costume and size, not in how they carry weight (Body, Sceptic): SEEN. Round's weight shows only in broad shapes.
- Round stand: arms bent and held away from the hips (Animator): SEEN, and it is characterful.
- Aliveness items (breath, timing, arcs, tiredness): CANNOT BE TOLD from stills; all three judges agree.

## Findings that stand, most serious first
1. The bear crawl reads as a pike or arch with the head buried, not a crawl; worst on Small (headless from front/back) and Long (fit 0.40, stilt limbs); lift the head and shorten the fold.
2. Long's coat lies out flat on the ground in the flat squat and bundles into a dark block in the half-kneel and hands and knees; the coat needs to follow the legs or be moved away from them.
3. Sitting up: the head tips back limp with no neck holding it, arms and legs dead straight on all three; nothing shows effort even at 0.56 on Small.
4. Rising from the half-kneel: the same upright, inert-armed stride on all three, rear leg stretched far behind; needs forward lean and some arm part.
5. Unloaded arms hang straight and the same in every stance; hands could rest on knees or thighs (a design choice for Luis, since arms are meant to rest).
6. Squat flat and squat on toes look the same; the toe stance cannot be told apart by eye.
7. The three miners differ in costume, not in carrying weight; Round's heaviness and Long's length show nowhere in the posture.
8. Round's flat squat: apron plank between the knees, hands out wide off the ground.

## What no judge looked at
- Round bear crawl shows a strip of bare shirt at the front; the bear crawl also leaves no sign of the hands being half a leg ahead; the posture looks like hands under shoulders.
- Small's lantern is hidden under her in the sit-up (the glow shows under the torso), and its light is the only sign.

**These stand** (items 1-8 above); the three stances that read as their own are tall kneel, hands and knees, and stand.
