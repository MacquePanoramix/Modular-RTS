# Body judge: drawn stances (Nov 5)

## Ranked findings
1. Bear crawl (hands and feet D=0.50), all three, worst Small and Round: trunk hangs as a collapsed arch, head hangs between the arms below the shoulders, face hidden; Small's coat covers the head/back view (looks headless), Long's pick and Small's hair/collar clip. Long fit 0.40 (poor): legs nearly straight, hips very high, heels up; reads as a toe-touch fold, not a bear crawl. Arms vertical with hands pressed at the same spot as the head. Reserve is tiny (0.05-0.08) yet the pose looks limp rather than propped: weight on the hands, trunk dangles. Fairly sure.
2. Squat flat, Long (fit 0.49, borderline poor): knees pushed to chest, coat tail splays on the ground behind (back view: coat fanned like a puddle), hands brush the boots; heels look lifted though "flat". Round front: apron/belly bunched between knees, head sunk, almost no face. Small: hands on the boot tops, reads fine.
3. Squat toes: all three near-identical to squat flat; difference barely visible. Hands touch the ground/boots, arms hang; Long and Round have no visible toe/heel difference from the sides. Repetition across both squats.
4. Half-kneel, Long: front view shows coat bundled into a black block between the knees with the mug floating beside it; the forward thigh is hidden. Back toes "laid" but the back foot points sideways in the Small/Round back views (ankle twist, toes outward). Small: forward knee clips into the coat.
5. Rising half-kneel: all three are the same lunge, rear knee a few cm off ground, arms hanging at the sides. Stiff and robotic: torso upright, no arm counterbalance; Long's front-view stance is very narrow in line (one foot straight in front of the other, like a tightrope); Small's lantern hangs oddly beside the knee. Weight (base +48 to +74 mm) is over the front foot, OK.
6. Sit-up propped on hands behind: Small reserve 0.56 (heavy) and she is shown with arms splayed and nearly straight, head dropped back, body like a doll; Long and Round lie nearly flat, hands behind reaching far out, head tilted back looking at the sky (neck extended, no chin tuck: nothing lifts the head). Back view of Long/Round: bulky heap, shoulders hunched around the head. Heels down, legs dead straight: no knee bend, dead-limp legs.
7. Hands and knees: good for all; Small/Long front views: coat/knees fully hidden; Round's lantern glow under the hand is nice. Long shows tiny knee spread; head hangs low, neck stretched (reads like a dog sniffing, acceptable). Long's mug hangs plausibly.
8. Stand and tall kneel: read as real. Tall kneel arms hang, shins laid, weight over knees; Long's tall kneel is well done. Stand: Small's lantern/hand right; Long's neck extends forward slightly.

## Across stances
- Arms: nearly always hang straight at the sides when not bearing weight (rests as intended, but in squats/rising they dangle with identical straight dead shape; no elbow bend, no wrist softness).
- Same torso lean pattern in the squats and half-kneel for every miner; the three miners differ mainly in costume and size, not in how they carry the pose. Round's heavy build is seen only in the sit-up (reserve 0.34) and nothing in the posture shows weight (no belly rest on thighs, no wider base). Long's length shows as a fold, not as long limbs placed with care.
- Heads: always look down/forward at the ground; same neck angle in each stance.
- Faces are hidden in many bends (Round squat front, Small bear crawl); the head reads as dropped.

## More their own
- Small: big head leads, hands wide for a short reach; use the heavy head to tip forward and a wider base.
- Long: knees splay wide and the trunk folds between thighs; elbows out; heels up in a deep squat is hard for long legs, let hands go to the ground as supports.
- Round: belly onto thighs, wide-set feet, hands on knees to push, slower, more arm support in rising.
