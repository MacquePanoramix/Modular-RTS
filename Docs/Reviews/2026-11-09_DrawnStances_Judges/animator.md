## Across stances (repetition/robotic)
- Resting arms are the same in every stance: straight, hanging, equal on both sides, fingers curled the same. This is the biggest repetition: no stance has an arm shifted, bent, a hand on a thigh or knee. Real bodies rest a hand on the thigh in a squat/half-kneel, elbow on the knee.
- Left/right symmetry: tall kneel, squat, sit-up are near perfectly mirror symmetric from the front. Heads all dead level in the front views (Long, Round tall kneel), no tilt, no gaze direction; gaze is generally straight down or ahead with no stance-fitted look.
- Head and neck: no stance shows the head adapting as a body does (looking at the ground to place hands, looking forward on rising); only in the pike and the squat does it drop.
- Hair/clothes: coats lie as static flat shapes; Long's long coat is the most often wrong (slabs, flaps). Small's coat is mostly tidy.
- Same family of look across stances: all low-effort stances look equally calm (faces blank); nothing shows effort even at S 0.56.

## Per miner: what would make it more its own
- Small: big head should weigh forward in the crouches and pike (the head could drop lower and the neck compensates); short legs make the lunge and squat tighter; lantern swings/hangs and bag rests on the ground in squat and sit-up.
- Long: its length is the problem, long coat, pick and mug are not accounted for (they stick out or bunch); knees and elbows should fold more, with hands resting on knees; the arms are too straight, and a lean lanky body would rise on a deeper forward lean. Long's fit numbers (0.35 to 0.49) are visible as stiffness.
- Round: the heavy trunk should sit lower on the thighs in squats and put the hands on the knees or ground for support; apron hangs in front like a plank, a visible belly/apron fold would help; arms flung wide in squats read as balancing for something not there.
