## What stands, most serious first

1. **The bear crawl** reads as a pike or an arch with the head buried, on all three: lift the head and shorten the fold
   (the stance's own definition, open).
2. **Long's coat** lies out flat on the ground in the deep squat and bundles into a dark block in the half-kneel and on
   hands and knees: the cloth must follow the legs (the drawing, open).
3. **Sitting up:** the head tips back limp, arms and legs dead straight; nothing shows effort even where Small needs 0.56 of
   its strength.
4. **Rising from the half-kneel:** the same upright, inert-armed stride on all three: a forward lean over the front knee and
   an arm's part are missing.
5. **The resting arms hang straight and alike in every stance** (the repetition Luis asked to be watched): hands could rest
   on the knees or thighs. Luis's to choose, since the rest itself was his (November 5).
6. **The squat on the toes cannot be told from the flat squat** by eye.

Not judged (stills cannot show it): breath, timing, arcs, tiredness. Their verdicts are not Luis's.
