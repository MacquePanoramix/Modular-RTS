# Standing by its own joints, with life in it: what its judges said — October 10, 2026

**What was judged.** Step 1 of [the plan](../NextMilestonePlan.md): a
miner standing at ease by what its own joints give, with breath, a
weight that goes from leg to leg, and a head that looks about
([the design](../Design/TheBodysOwn.md#step-1-the-rest-of-it-the-body-in-the-game-and-the-life-in-it-october-9-and-10)).

**Who.** Luis, October 9: "good job with the agent reviews, please
continue with that for further steps always". Two rounds; each round
three judges who did not make it and had seen nothing before (the body,
the animator, the sceptic: [their briefs](../../Art/Review/judges/README.md),
with [the eight things a living body does](../../Art/Review/judges/alive.md)).
They were given sheets of pictures of Small, Long and Round, what the
body was told to do, and figures from its trace. They were not told
what had been changed, nor what the judges before them said.

**A judge's verdict is not Luis's.** Nothing here is accepted.

## In short

- **Stable: three of three, both rounds.** Boots flat and planted in
  every picture; no step, slide or sink; nudged, nothing snaps. "Each
  looks like it could be frozen in any picture and stay up."
- **Alive only in its head: three of three, both rounds.** "A still
  body with a roving head." Its breath could not be seen, at rest or
  tired, at two and a half times the size of life (round 1) or at four
  times (round 2). Its weight going over a leg could not be seen: no
  judge could tell which leg it stood on.
- **Its head turns alone** ("a turret"): nothing leads the turn, and
  nothing below the neck follows it. Three of three in round 2.
- **Three things that are the models' and not this step's** came up
  each time: Long carries its chin up; Round's and Long's arms hang
  away from their bodies in an A; Round's eyes are shut and no miner
  blinks.

So the second half of what Luis asked for ("natural and alive") is not
there yet below the neck, and I do not think more of the same will
bring it: see [what is open](#what-is-open).

## What is open

1. **Its breath.** Drawn on the chest it is not apparent, at any size
   that still looks like a chest. Luis's answer B4 foresaw this ("If
   not then we can think of other ways to represent it"). Other ways,
   none built: the shoulders and collar rising, drawn boldly; a tired
   body heaving (its trunk bowing and coming up with each breath);
   breath seen in the cold air at dusk; or left as it is in life,
   hardly seen. For Luis to choose, with the three looks on the panel
   to begin from.
2. **Its weight going over a leg** cannot be made large enough to read
   until it can step (the plan's step 3): standing further over a leg,
   a light nudge took it over.
3. **Its head turning alone.** The chest following a far look is to be
   put back and judged; eyes that move, and a blink, are not built.
4. **How these characters are made to stand** (Long's chin, the arms in
   an A, Round's shut eyes) is seen more now that the body is otherwise
   still and looked at closely. For Luis: is it meant?
5. **The plan's order.** Steps 2 (knees onto feet) and 3 (a step) are in
   that order; what this step found (leaning, a real shift of weight,
   and standing up to a push all wait on the step) speaks for the step
   first. Luis's to say.
