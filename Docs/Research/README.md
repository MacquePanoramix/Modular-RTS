# Research

What was looked up outside the project, so that a decision inside it
rests on something. Each page says where each thing comes from and how
far it can be trusted. A page here is a reference, not a decision:
nothing is Locked by being written in it.

| Page | What it is for |
|---|---|
| [What real bodies do, and how to judge ours](2026-10-08_RealBodiesAndHowToJudgeThem.md) (October 8, 2026) | For the playtest round's ten notes: fireflies; shadows far from the camera; walking; turning round; clothes in a bend; going down for a thing; getting up; resting; reaching and holding; what makes a movement read as real; and the ways of checking a movement, among them judges that did not make it |

| [Alive, and the body's own](2026-10-09_AliveAndTheBodysOwn.md) (October 9, 2026) | For Luis's direction of October 9: how long a fallen body lies; what makes a movement read as alive and not robotic (what animators say, and what studies of seeing movement say), set against the miners in eight points; how a body is moved by its own joints and kept stable; and, action by action (standing, setting off, walking, turning, lifting, swinging, falling), what a real body does and how it is made to read |
| [How people get up, and how getting up is shown](2026-10-14_HowPeopleGetUp.md) (October 14, 2026) | For Luis's request of October 14, before the miners' arms are made stronger: how real people (adults, older people, children, fighters, dancers) get up, how viewers see weight in movement, how animation and anime show it, how games and simulated bodies do it; and the twelve things our getting up is held to |
| [Getting up for any body](reports/Getting%20up%20for%20any%20body.md) (October 30, 2026) | For Luis's request of October 30: a getting up for any body the creator can make, natural and procedural. Six threads (learned control across bodies; learned getting up and what makes it natural; planning and designed controllers; the causes of twitches and broken-looking limbs; how build and strength change how people rise; references to study frame by frame and motion data with their licences), 153 sources; recommends a capacity-driven procedural rise, built from a body that cannot look broken. Its notes: [research_notes/Getting up for any body](research_notes/Getting%20up%20for%20any%20body/). The October 22 report before it: [Getting up by its own joints](reports/Getting%20up%20by%20its%20own%20joints.md) |
| [Breaking rock, and tiredness: what feels right](2026-10-14_MiningAndTiredness.md) (October 14, 2026) | For Luis's request of October 14: how real hand miners and heavy workers paced themselves, tired and rested; what tiredness does to strength and precision; how games, film and animation show mining and tiredness; and what the miners' work is held to (W1 to W8) |
