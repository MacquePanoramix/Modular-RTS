Working notes for the harness-guide review. Source: the package (another person's private work; nothing copied,

## 1. New principles not in P1 to P36

- **N1 Search before you ask.** The other director was asked 24 questions whose answers were mostly already written
  in his project, and said that was a bad sign. Before asking the director, look in what he already said; ask an
  already-answered point as "you said X; still true?". Where: intent, "concern" and "answer status". Guards against:
  wasted director time, and a record nobody can find. Here: applies. Search Correspondence and memory first.
- **N2 "Decide it" or "I don't know" is an answer, not missing homework.** Record it as delegated; never re-ask it as
  if blank. Where: intent, answer status (Q9, Q11, Q15). Here: applies; matches "delegated calls recorded not Locked".
- **N3 Explain first; options only when asked.** When a choice needs him, he wants the problem and its effect on the
  game told plainly; he asks for alternatives himself. Where: intent Q18. Guards against: option menus as homework.
  Here: a style choice, not a rule. Luis likes to see what each option looks like, so offer pictures when they help.
- **N4 Research privately before raising a conflict.** A clash with an accepted idea reaches the director only once
  the agent has a solid basis. Where: intent Q6, Q16; strategy last section. Guards against: vague worries handed up.
  Here: applies; fits "research first".
- **N5 Doubt with evidence, or not yet.** Raise a weakness when there is enough to build something better; but thin
  evidence is never shown as settled, and strong contrary evidence is never hidden. Where: intent Q5 and the
  interpretations. Here: applies to "does this movement look real" doubts.
- **N6 Loose talk becomes work only when the director promotes it.** Ideas are kept and can be brought back when they
  become relevant, but relevant is not adopted. Where: intent Q8, Q14. Guards against: a passing idea silently
  becoming a feature. Here: applies; matches Open/Possible/Direction.
- **N7 A temporary assumption must not quietly become permanent.** One of his biggest worries, above all ones he
  cannot track himself. Where: intent Q3. Here: applies. Keep a short list of working assumptions, each with when it
  is checked again.
- **N8 A causal timeline you can open step by step** is what he wants for any surprise. Where: intent Q19. Here:
  applies; frame-by-frame reads already do it. A one-line "this caused that" in reports would help Luis.
- **N9 Keep the director's words apart from the agent's reading of them.** The page marks which lines he confirmed
  and which are the assistant's interpretation. Where: intent, interpretations. Close to P18, applied to his answers.
- **N10 Checks run with each piece, not saved for the end.** Stages are kinds of work, not sealed gates; the real
  connections are checked as each piece lands. Where: strategy, "four steps" and the order table. Guards against:
  all problems surfacing at the end. Here: applies; matches focused tests per change.
- **N11 Use the new way on real work early.** Put a small real task through a new process or tool as soon as it
  works, to find where it does not fit. Where: strategy, package F "start early". Here: applies to any new process
  idea from this review: try it on the next real step before writing it in as a rule.
- **N12 A small probe before a large build.** When a design hinges on an unknown, run a small test to settle it
  rather than building big on a guess. Where: strategy, "recommended direction" and "minimize rework". Here: applies
  (short sim searches before a full change).
- **N13 Reproduce a suspected fault before fixing it.** A suspected risk gets reproduced and then fixed or rejected
  with evidence; a rejected false alarm is kept so nobody "repairs" it later. Where: strategy, last tables. Guards
  against: fixes for problems that do not exist. Here: applies; fits "count the energy first".
- **N14 Keep negative results so they are not redone.** No new research just to repeat a result already found
  negative. Where: strategy, package F. Here: applies; Validation.md prior failures already serve this.
- **N15 Reuse must name the work it saves.** Reusing a tool or a past result is justified by the exact job it removes
  and what it keeps true. A product's documents show what it can do, not that it fits this project. Where: strategy,
  "order and economical reuse". Here: applies to new Unity packages and tools.
- **N16 A generic tool's score is not your bar.** An off-the-shelf checker may count a time-out as a catch; a
  project-specific check with its own clear bar beats a borrowed score. Where: strategy, the mutation section. Here:
  applies to borrowed quality metrics for movement or visuals.
- **N17 An optional improvement must not become a condition for finishing.** Where: strategy, "recommended direction"
  and "keep orchestration optional". Guards against: the finish line moving. Here: applies.
- **N18 Close dead issues on purpose.** An obsolete worry is closed in writing, not left hanging. Where: strategy,
  "residual disciplines". Here: applies to the Outlook and Handoff lists. Close to P10.
- **N19 An honest "still blocked" can be a correct finish of an assessment.** Where: strategy, package B. Close to
  P11. Applies.
- **N20 The director's real cost: no extra paid services and no repeated tinkering.** The system should fix ordinary
  technical trouble itself. Where: intent Q23. Here: applies; matches the usage budget.
- Not new enough to list: fresh-copy proof before retiring an old one (P10, P28); "only what depends on it waits" (P34).

## 3. Against heavy process, and what cost too much

- Intent: the reform must keep the way of working that already worked, not replace it with paperwork or with human
  process management. He says most of his problems started with the process reform, not with making the game.
- Intent: he does not want to be reviewer or operator; anything far from the game's content he would rather skip.
- Intent Q15: refusing every simplification is named as why the reform has been so hard to finish.
- Intent: the questionnaire itself repeated what was already written; a costly sign of a record nobody could find.
- Strategy: cut rework, time and tokens; no planning of the whole future game, no repeated reviews or tests of
  unchanged work, no tools or paperwork without a shown net gain; no new cleanup service, no deletion quota.
- Strategy: optional tools stay optional; a new orchestration tool is not adopted unless it saves real work.
- Observation (ours): the strategy page is itself a warning. It is so dense with internal labels, counts and
  sub-cases that it is close to unreadable for a newcomer. Plain words and short entry pages matter (P24).

## 4. How that director wants to be involved, and what annoys him

- Pattern: brainstorm with the lead agent, mixing his ideas, history research and AI suggestions; then the AI works
  on its own and calls him only for a real director choice or something unexpected.
- He wants: short causal stories, comparisons, short recommendations, and how it changes the actual game.
- Questions: specific, about a real choice, after research; never a repeated questionnaire. Technical supply and
  permissions are the AI's work, "not a questionnaire" for him (strategy, last table).
- Most of all: no silent changes of meaning.
- Annoyed by: being asked what is already written; technical homework; process topics; tinkering with the tools;
  paying for extra services. He also worried his answers were contradictory or too open; reading them back to him
  as confirmed versus interpreted (N9) answers that worry.
- Same as Luis: good questions, no homework, care about the content. Different: Luis also judges feel and looks
  himself in play, so he is involved at playtests, not only at brainstorms.
