# The essence beyond the surface

**Started:** October 2, 2026, after Luis's reply to the hand-painted pass
([correspondence](../Correspondence/2026-10-02_THE_ESSENCE_BEYOND_THE_SURFACE.md)).
**Status:** research and working hypotheses for stage S1e. Nothing here is
Locked. **The first iteration was not adopted:** Luis preferred the
hand-painted pass. See the next section. The [Visual Soul](VisualSoul.md) stays the art direction; this
document looks for what lies underneath it.

Luis asked for more than a look: the breathtaking, out-of-this-world emotion
of "the image from my mind's eye straight out of a dream". He sent seven
images from or after Studio Ghibli films (Howl's Moving Castle and Spirited
Away). They are described in the correspondence and are not committed,
because they are copyrighted film frames.

Luis was clear: the essence, not the surface. So this document
does not try to imitate Ghibli. It asks **why** these images produce that
feeling, and what that means for a 3D strategy game.

## Luis's verdict, and what Luis's favourite frame teaches

**The verdict, October 2.** The same day, after seeing the first iteration's
captures, Luis preferred "the before" (the hand-painted pass), and attached a frame
taken in that build and "absolutely loved".
- **The base.** The hand-painted pass stays the base.
- **The first iteration** (sections 3 to 5, and
  [TheEssencePlaytest.md](../Playtests/TheEssencePlaytest.md)) is not adopted.
  Its code is kept on the branch `claude/essence-exploration`.

**His frame** is look E at dusk (19:12), seen from low on the path in front
of the house. Read against the first iteration, it says:

| His frame | What the first iteration did instead |
|---|---|
| **Intimacy.** The lit house is the whole picture, seen close and from below. | Vistas: a lake, ranges and a horizon |
| **The ordinary moment.** Warm light pours from an open door and windows into a cool dusk. That is reference A, and the handoff's first line: "even a house with its lights on in a grassland at night should be beautiful and magical". | Wonder from scale and spectacle |
| **A soft, brushed sky.** Thin, wispy, lilac clouds. | Monumental, sculpted cumulus |
| **Enclosure.** Soft dark hills hold the place like cupped hands; the world feels small and safe. | A world opened out to the horizon |
| **A tonal palette.** Everything shares the dusk's hue. | Bright, saturated daylight (cerulean, yellow-green) |
| **Tender, strange scale.** Tall dark grass blades loom in the foreground (reference D). | Kept |

**The lesson.** For Luis, the breathtaking feeling lives in the quiet, warm,
enclosed moment: the pocket of life rather than the immensity around it. The
Ghibli references were read too literally, as vastness and brightness.

**What was missing from the process.** The first iteration replaced the look
Luis loved instead of adding options beside it, and it was not shown to Luis
early. From now on:
- **Explorations are additive and switchable.** Nothing Luis loves is replaced
  by default.
- **Big swings are shown early**, as a few frames, before they are built
  out.

**Kept from this study, if Luis wants them** (each would be an option over
the before, never a replacement):
- **Fireflies** around the lit house at dusk and night. They fit Luis's frame.
- **Composed viewpoints** on keys.
- **Look F's painting pass and ink on people only**, as an extra look.
- **The research method:** measuring colour against references.

## 1. What the references share

Looking across all seven images, nine qualities recur. The first four carry
most of the emotion.

### The world is bigger than you

Every image opens onto a horizon: mountains, a lake, the sea, a sky that
fills half the frame.

- The house, the train or the two small figures sit inside an immensity.
- The feeling is smallness held tenderly by a vast, kind world.
- Reference F of the Visual Soul says the same: "great distances make the
  pocket of life precious".

**The Ordinary Place today** is a hill ringed by fog. The world ends about
200 metres away.

### The sky is the protagonist

The clouds are architecture:

- towering cumulus as large as mountains;
- warm, lit crowns and lilac-grey bellies;
- flat bases receding in rows toward the horizon.

The sky sets the mood of everything under it, and is painted with as much
care as any house.

**Today** our clouds are thin wisps on the dome, and the Strategy camera
never shows the sky.

### The light is luminous, and the air is visible

The images are high-key. Midtones are bright and whites are real.

- **Shadows.** They are colour (lilac, blue-green), never darkness.
- **Distance.** It is painted with air: each farther layer is lighter,
  bluer and softer, until mountains nearly dissolve into the sky.
- **Cloud shadows.** Shafts of sunlight and shadows of clouds cross the land
  and give it rhythm.

**Today** our captures are about 30% darker than the daylight references
(measured below). The aerial depth stops at one layer of fogged hills.

### The colour is chosen, not lit

Kazuo Oga, Ghibli's background director for Totoro, describes the aim as
"happy colors… colors that make you feel relieved".

- **Meadows.** They are a sunlit yellow-green.
- **Skies and water.** They are cerulean and turquoise.
- **Accents.** Flowers in lilac, pink, orange and white are scattered as
  small accents.

The palette is saturated but harmonious.

**Today** our greens are cooler and our sky is paler and more lavender (see
the measurements).

### Water doubles the sky

Four of the seven images hold water. A mirror-still sea, a lake and winding
streams reflect the sky. In Spirited Away's flooded plain, sky and sea become
one dream.

**For a strategy game this matters most of all.** From a high camera, water
and cloud shadows are how the sky reaches the ground.

### The wind moves the world

Clouds drift, grass waves in gusts and smoke leans. Wind runs through all of
Miyazaki's films.

**Today** our grass sways, but you cannot see a gust travel across the
meadow.

### Ma: room to breathe

The frames are uncluttered.

- **Calm areas.** Large quiet areas of sky, water and meadow let the eye
  rest.
- **One accent.** A single small accent of life: a house, a train or a
  figure.
- **Ma.** Miyazaki calls the pause *ma*, "the time in between my clapping":
  it is where the emotion grows.

### Real places, observed truly, made kinder

Oga's backgrounds are specific: particular trees, the way light changes a
pillar's colour. They are also simplified into painted masses, with detail
gathered where the eye lands.

- **His test.** "I'd probably feel good if I were in a place like this, even
  on my own."
- **The architecture.** Howl's half-timbered towns come from real Alsace,
  then made warmer.

### The impossible, presented as ordinary

A walking castle, a train across the sea, a house alone on an islet in a
flooded world. The wonder is shown with the same calm, believable light as
the everyday, which is why it feels like a dream rather than a spectacle.

### Painted backgrounds, drawn characters

In every frame the landscape has no outlines: it is gouache, with soft
clouds and crisp roofs. The people are drawn with lines and flat shading.
The difference makes the figures readable and the world deep.

**Today** look E puts ink on everything near the camera, landscape included.

## 2. Measurements

Colour statistics of Luis's seven images against our hand-painted captures
(look E). They were computed on downscaled copies.

- **Hue.** The median hue of saturated greens and blues.
- **Brightness.** HSV value and luminance percentiles.

| Image | Green hue | Green brightness | Blue hue | Blue saturation | Share of blue (sky/water) | Median luminance |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Castle in the highlands | 76° | 0.71 | 189° | 0.41 | 53% | 0.57 |
| 2. Cottage among flower rivers | 72° | 0.49 | 191° | 0.57 | 23% | 0.52 |
| 3. Lakeside picnic | 61° | 0.60 | 219° | 0.44 | 31% | 0.45 |
| 5. Sea railway (day frame first) | 96° | 0.77 | 200° | 0.39 | 38% | 0.65 |
| **Ours, path by day** | **100°** | **0.35** | **215°** | **0.29** | **21%** | **0.46** |
| **Ours, overview by day** | **99°** | **0.33** | — | — | **1%** | **0.31** |

**What the numbers say:**

1. **Our grass is half as bright and cooler.** It sits at about 100° against
   the references' 60–76° yellow-green.
2. **Our sky is a paler, more violet blue.** It sits at 215° against their
   cerulean 189–200°, and is less saturated.
3. **The sky barely appears.** Sky and water fill 23–53% of a daylight
   reference, 21% of our ground-level view and 1% of our Strategy view.
4. **There is no bright end.** The references reach luminance 0.84–0.91 at
   the 90th percentile (sunlit cloud and water). Ours reaches about 0.65.

The dusk and night frames need no such correction: ours are already in the
references' range.

**After the first iteration.** The same measures were taken again; see the
table in [TheEssencePlaytest.md](../Playtests/TheEssencePlaytest.md#measured).
- **Green.** 83–87° and brightness 0.52–0.54.
- **Sky and water.** Hue 194–195° and saturation 0.43–0.50, filling 26–32% of
  ground-level views and 69% at the lake viewpoint.
- **Median luminance.** 0.58–0.65.

All are now inside or at the edge of the references' range. The Strategy
camera still sees almost no sky.

## 3. What this means for Wonder Gather

These are hypotheses. Each is tested in the engine and judged by Luis.

1. **Build the beyond.**
   - **The far world.** Around the meadow: a valley, a large lake opening
     toward a water horizon, and layered mountain ranges with one snow peak.
     The playable meadow stays the same size.
   - **The beyond is scenery.** It is never walkable, only seen.
2. **Make the sky monumental.**
   - **The clouds.** Real 3D cumulus towers modelled in Blender and painted
     by a dedicated shader: warm crowns, lilac bellies, banded light and a
     silver lining toward the sun.
   - **Their colour.** It follows the time of day, from noon white to sunset
     apricot to night blue.
3. **Let the sky reach the ground.**
   - **Cloud shadows.** They drift across the land.
   - **Still water.** It mirrors the clouds and mountains (real planar
     reflections).
   - **The Strategy camera.** It sees the sky through both.
4. **Paint the air.**
   - **Aerial perspective.** It takes the sky's own horizon colour, so far
     mountains melt into the sky.
   - **Light and colour.** A brighter, high-key day with coloured shadows.
5. **Choose the colours.**
   - **Meadow.** Shift it to a luminous yellow-green.
   - **Sky.** Shift it to cerulean, with whiter clouds.
   - **Flowers.** Scatter them as accents (iteration 3, below).
6. **Show the wind.** Gusts travel visibly across the meadow (iteration 3).
7. **Keep ma.** Large calm areas, and one small warm accent of life.
8. **Paint the landscape, draw the people.** Built in the first iteration as
   look F (see device 1 below).
9. **The impossible thing.** Left to Luis, since the world setting is still
   Open. Options are in device 6 below.

## 4. Wonder Gather's own language

Luis's second message the same day sets the bar: the Visual Soul is the
artistic soul, and the result must go above and beyond it, to something
"truly unique to this Wonder Gather game". His first message adds the frame
test: every frame the camera lands on should be a painting, driven by light,
while the game still runs well as an RTS.

So the references give principles, never a look to copy. What follows is
Claude's proposal for a language that belongs to Wonder Gather alone. It is
a **Direction to test, not Locked**; Luis decides what stays.

### The thesis

> Warm lives, drawn by hand, in a breathing painted world that is always
> bigger than the frame.

Everything small and human is warm and drawn. Everything vast is painted,
cool and moving. Light passes between them like a gift. This joins the six
soul images:
- A and D: pools of warmth;
- B and E: drawn people;
- C: living daylight;
- F: a cared-for pocket in immensity.

It also keeps Luis's influences in view:
- **Ghibli:** painted light and the ordinary moment;
- **Little Nightmares:** strange scale and tactile dark;
- **Ranking of Kings:** expressive drawing;
- **Mario Galaxy:** cosmic wonder.

### Seven signature devices

| # | Device | What it is | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Two hands: drawn lives, painted world** | **People and units are drawn:** lively contours and confident flat shapes, after B, E and Luis's own reference. **The world is painted:** no outlines, strokes along the forms. **The RTS benefit:** units read instantly against the land, so art and readability come from one device. | Built in look F (ink on characters only) |
| 2 | **The painting follows the forms** | Every pixel is painted with strokes laid along the shapes it belongs to. The strokes are broader with distance and finest where the eye lands. The frame reads as one painting, not as a filtered 3D render. | Built in look F (the painting pass) |
| 3 | **The painting breathes** | Next, the strokes in grass, water and clouds also lean with the wind. The whole canvas then moves like a living painting. | Proposed |
| 4 | **The air is alive with light** | **Seeds and pollen** glow only when you look towards the sun. **Fireflies** blink over the meadow at dusk and night. **Next:** warm motes and a painted glow around lamplight. Light becomes something you can see moving through the air. | Seeds and fireflies built; lamplight proposed |
| 5 | **Every hour has its palette** | **Like a painter's paints for one canvas,** each hour has a chosen shade hue and light hue: cerulean and cream at noon, lilac and peach at golden hour, indigo and amber at night. **The method:** shadows stay saturated in their own hue and only lean towards the shade hue; they never go grey. | Built in look F (the grade) |
| 6 | **A pocket of warmth in immensity** | **The world is always bigger than the frame:** monumental clouds, water that doubles the sky, and ranges dissolving into air. **Beyond that, F's impossibility:** the horizon could curve gently like the edge of a small world, and the night could open into a deep painted cosmos, making the Ordinary Place a cared-for pocket on an immense world. **Why it waits:** world setting is Open, so this needs Luis. | The beyond built; the curving world and cosmic night proposed |
| 7 | **Tender, strange scale** | **Close to the ground,** grass, flowers and reeds loom as in D. **From the bluff,** the world is vast. **The Explore camera** carries the player between the two, and the Strategy camera keeps the game. | Partly built (Explore camera, tall grass); giant flowers and reeds proposed |

### The frame test

Any frame the camera lands on should have:

1. **Light you can read:** a direction, a source, or a pool of warmth.
2. **Air:** at least three layers of depth (near, middle, far), with air
   between them.
3. **One warm accent of life:** a window, a figure or a lantern.
4. **A calm area for the eye (ma):** sky, water or meadow.
5. **No 3D giveaways:** no faceted silhouettes, plastic highlights, flat
   untextured areas or shimmering edges.

Captures are judged against this list as well as against the references.

## 5. The exploration plan (stage S1e)

After Luis's follow-up messages, the rendering language became the main
track, alongside the world.

| Iteration | Content | Judged by |
|---|---|---|
| 1. The beyond and the painting | **World:** the far world, the lake with reflections, monumental clouds, cloud shadows, aerial perspective and a luminous palette. **Rendering:** look F with the painting pass, drawn people, the hour's palette and the living air. | matched captures, the frame test, the frame cost; Luis in the build |
| 2. Forms that read as paint | **Trees:** painted clumps with leaf-dab silhouettes instead of faceted blobs. **The land:** far fields and mountains painted rather than faceted. **Clouds:** brushed edges. **Grass:** strokes that breathe with the wind. | the same |
| 3. The meadow and the warm light | **The meadow:** visible gusts, flowers and a stream. **Warm light:** a painted glow and motes around lamplight. **Ma:** quiet moments. | the same |
| 4. The signature decisions | The proposals that need Luis: the curving world and cosmic night, and giant flowers and reeds. | Luis |

Every iteration is measured again with the same statistics and the frame
benchmark, and is reported with what was not tested.
